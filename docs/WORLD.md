# WARPPOINT — World Specification

## Setting
A hostile alien planet affected by an unstable warp phenomenon.

## Visual Language
Cinematic sci-fi, realistic materials, atmospheric depth, alien geology and high-contrast energy technology.

## Key Landmarks
### Crash Site
Player starting area and first navigation landmark.

### Alien Wilderness
Primary exploration and combat space.

### Research Facility
Future story and mission location; foundation asset for the vertical slice.

### Warp Extraction Point
High-visibility destination that communicates the mission endpoint through world design.
