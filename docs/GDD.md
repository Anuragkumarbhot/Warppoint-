# WARPPOINT — Game Design Document

## Vision
WARPPOINT is a third-person sci-fi survival shooter set on a hostile alien world after a catastrophic warp event.

## Core Loop
Explore → Loot → Combat → Survive → Extract

## Vertical Slice v0.1
1. Spawn at the crash site.
2. Explore alien terrain.
3. Find the Pulse Rifle.
4. Encounter and defeat a Xeno Stalker.
5. Collect a resource.
6. Reach the Warp Extraction Point.
7. Complete extraction.

## Player
- Third-person movement
- Camera/look
- Sprint and stamina
- Health
- Jump/crouch foundation
- Weapon handling

## Combat
### Pulse Rifle
Primary ranged weapon for the v0.1 slice.

### Xeno Stalker
Fast hostile creature with patrol, detection, chase, attack, damage and death states.

## World
- Crash Site
- Alien Wilderness
- Research Facility foundation
- Resource zones
- Warp Extraction Point

## UI
Minimal futuristic HUD: health, stamina, ammunition, weapon state and objective.

## Platforms
Desktop and mobile. Mobile controls are designed from the beginning, not added as a later port.

## Design Principle
Teach gameplay through level design, environmental cues and feedback first; use HUD instructions sparingly.
